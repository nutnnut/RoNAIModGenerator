Suspects who you are fighting

Suspect count
Multiplier scales what each map already has, so Hospital stays busier than Gas Station. Flat range puts the same min/max on every map. MAX is one number with no range at all — the game clamps it to the spawn points a map actually contains.
Enemy accuracy
Adjusts the aim cone together with range falloff and how long a suspect tracks you before firing — the things that actually decide whether you get hit.
Accuracy while moving
Suspects give up aim cone for every metre per second they are moving. Vanilla sets that penalty so low it is worth about 3% at a walking pace, which is why sprinting suspects still land shots. This is separate from the aim above: it only applies while their feet are moving.
Suspect awareness
How far they see and hear, how wide their cone of vision is, and how long they remember. Separate from accuracy: this decides whether they notice you at all.
Roaming suspects
Roamers hunt you across the whole map instead of holding a room. Vanilla allows 1–4 depending on difficulty, and a couple more changes a level completely.
Suspect health
Vanilla gives most maps their own health value. Keep that variation, or flatten it to one number everywhere.
Suspects giving up
Every AI carries a hidden morale value, rolled at spawn and drained by shouting, flashbangs, tasers and watching a friend go down. Low morale is what makes them surrender, so this sets where they start.
Less-lethal effectiveness
Stun health is a pool that flashbangs, tasers, beanbags and gas deplete; when it empties the AI goes down. This moves the pool and every source's damage together.

Civilians who you are there for

Civilian count
Fewer civilians means fewer judgement calls. Removing them entirely can trivialise objectives that ask you to rescue them.
Civilians complying
The same morale system, tuned separately. Vanilla civilians break more easily than suspects do.

SWAT your side of the door

Teammate health
How much your AI squad can take. This is SwatHealth, which covers the AI officers only — your own durability comes from the armour you pick, which lives outside the difficulty files.
Teammate skill
Aim cone, reaction time and how long they hold a target's last known position. Turn it down if you want the fight to be yours, up if you are playing solo against raised counts.

Environment the building itself

Traps
Wired doors. Only ten maps ship them in vanilla — everything else inherits a global setting that pre-places none.
Doors & keycards
What fraction of the level starts locked or already open. Locking the place down forces breaching tools and slows every approach.

Difficulties to rewrite

Pak file name
The _P suffix is what gives it priority over the shipping paks. Keep it.
Spawn placement
Trap details
Used by “All maps”. The game lists Explosive, Flashbang and Alarm globally; ToxicGas and TowerAlarm each appear on one map only and look map-specific, so adding them everywhere is a gamble.
Maps often redefine a global key for themselves, and the map-local value wins at runtime. With this on, keys you set here are rewritten inside those map sections too. Keys you did not touch keep their hand-tuned values either way.
Morale damage sources (MoraleDamageRate, PlayerYellMorale.Damage, FlashMorale.Damage, TaserMorale.Damage…) hit suspects and civilians alike, so the two controls above deliberately leave them alone. Add them below if you want to move both at once.
Structural keys such as SquadsEnabled and UseSpawnGroups are best left alone: forcing them on a map that ships them off can stop it spawning anyone.
Extra global settings
Start typing and the box filters every setting, each with its category, its stock value and a description. Descriptions come from community documentation of the AI options; the value and whether a key is set at all come from the game you have installed. A few are documented but not written by the stock file — the game still reads them. Applied last, so anything here overrides the controls above.
